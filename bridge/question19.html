Security Information

Why turning off autocomplete can improve security:

  • Prevents data storage: When autocomplete is disabled, browsers will not store or suggest previously entered sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.
  • Protects on shared computers: On public or shared computers, disabling autocomplete prevents other users from seeing previously entered sensitive information.
  • Reduces information leakage: It prevents accidental disclosure of sensitive data through browser suggestions or auto-fill features.
  • Compliance requirements: Many security standards and regulations recommend or require disabling autocomplete for forms containing sensitive information.
  • Prevents shoulder surfing: Auto-suggestions can make sensitive information visible to people nearby when typing.

Note: All form fields in this example use the required attribute to ensure data validation and prevent form submission with empty fields.
